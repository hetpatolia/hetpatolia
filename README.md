# 💫 About Me:
I work in Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) with a focus on ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and information security auditing. My current emphasis is on understanding how security controls operate in practice — not just how they are documented — and how risk treatment decisions align with business objectives. I approach compliance as a risk-driven discipline, not a checklist exercise.<br><br>I am developing hands-on capability in ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditing, internal audits, control effectiveness evaluation, and evidence-based assurance. Alongside this, I am expanding my knowledge across complementary compliance and security frameworks to understand control mapping, overlap, and gaps. My goal is to contribute to security programs that can withstand scrutiny from auditors, regulators, and real-world threats.<br><br>I am open to collaborating in cybersecurity and GRC-focused initiatives, particularly where governance, risk management, and technical security intersect. I value direct communication, structured thinking, and accountability — because in security, assumptions fail and evidence matters.
