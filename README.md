### CyberRisk Nexus — Evidence-Driven Cyber Risk Decision, Governance and Assurance Intelligence Platform (Under Development)

### ControlDrift — Continuous Security Control Drift, Evidence Validity and Assurance Revalidation Engine (Under Development)

A security-assurance system focused on determining how environmental changes affect security controls, previously collected evidence, audit assertions, risk exposure, and required revalidation.

Core concept:

### TRACE-Cyber — An Evidence-Driven Threat-to-Control Traceability Model for Continuous Cybersecurity Assurance (Under Development)

An academic cybersecurity research project investigating whether fragmented security evidence can be systematically connected across vulnerability assessment, security operations, incident response, digital forensics, risk management, security controls, compliance, remediation, and continuous assurance.

Core research chain:

`Asset → Exposure → Vulnerability → Threat → Attack Technique → Detection → Incident → Forensic Evidence → Root Cause → Risk → Security Control → Compliance Requirement → Remediation → Retesting → Control Effectiveness → Continuous Assurance`

## 📚 Research Interests

My research interests are centered around evidence-driven cybersecurity assurance, cyber risk, security controls, GRC, digital forensics, and the relationship between technical security evidence and organizational assurance.

### TRACE-Cyber

**TRACE-Cyber: An Evidence-Driven Threat-to-Control Traceability Model for Continuous Cybersecurity Assurance**

The research investigates the fragmentation of cybersecurity evidence and proposes an empirically evaluated approach for connecting technical findings, threats, attack techniques, security telemetry, incidents, forensic evidence, risks, controls, compliance requirements, remediation, retesting, and control effectiveness.

The research is designed as a genuine academic and empirical cybersecurity study involving a controlled laboratory evaluation, measurable metrics, baseline comparison, and a retrospective real-world case study.

### Operationalization Framework for RBI FREE-AI in Regulated Financial Institutions

**Operationalization framework for RBI FREE-AI in regulated financial institutions**

This white paper focuses on translating RBI's Framework for Responsible and Ethical Enablement of AI (FREE-AI) into practical governance mechanisms, control objectives, evidence requirements, assurance processes, audit checkpoints, lifecycle governance, and implementation guidance for regulated financial institutions.

The work considers alignment with established sources including ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OECD AI Principles, and financial-sector governance expectations.
