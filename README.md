# Hi, I'm Het Patoliya 👋

### Governance, Risk & Compliance | Information Security | Cybersecurity

I am an M.Sc. Digital Forensics & Information Security student at the National Forensic Sciences University (NFSU), Gandhinagar, with a focus on Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), information security assurance, cybersecurity, and security testing.

## 🧩 What I Work On

I am particularly interested in the intersection of governance and technical security.

My work focuses on connecting business risk, security controls, technical evidence, compliance requirements, remediation, and continuous assurance rather than treating compliance as a checklist exercise.

I am currently building practical cybersecurity projects and research-oriented systems covering cyber risk governance, security control monitoring, threat-to-control traceability, and evidence-driven security assurance.
